# Decisions, 2026-10-31 onward

| Date | Decision |
|---|---|
| 2026-10-31 | **A first native iOS prototype** (the user: "a real native iOS app, not just a web app in the shell of an app... start with something small like a simple battle map with a few characters and different elements and the core party piece"). Swift + SpriteKit, as tech.md §13 planned, in `ios/` (XcodeGen, bundle `com.jonathancox.ninthtower`; landscape, 120 Hz on ProMotion). **The pipeline it proves:** the art is exported, not redrawn: `tools/native-export.js` runs in the dev page, boots the Training Grounds (`arena`) and writes every sheet the slice draws (the party's baked gear sheets and portraits, five foes, effects, icons, the pixel font) with its frames and animations, the map's painted ground, its 399 props as placed (with their depth) and its walkable grid, and renders 22 sound effects through the web synth offline; `node tools/native-sync.mjs` unpacks that into `ios/NinthTower/Export/` (1 MB) with heroes, enemies, skills, items and tuning.json copied unchanged. **What's ported** (line for line where it could be, in the web's own space, pixels with y down): the leader's commands and idle attacks, the companions' targeting by role, formation follow, flanking, ranged spacing and firing spots, dodging locked telegraphs, reviving; foes noticing, surrounding with two attack tokens, wind-ups that track then lock, archers keeping away and shifting after a shot; damage, mitigation, crits, evasion and the counter, elements (weak/resist, WEAK!, burn, poison, chill stacking to freeze); Brann's Bash and Taunt, Pip's Fireball and Mend, Sable's Shadow Step and Fan of Knives; the three ultimates; hit-stop, shake, the game speed (0.72), A* with string-pulling. **The battle:** waves on the Training Grounds, each a mix of elements (goblins and an archer; the frost pack; ember imps and gloom spiders; all of them), foes two levels up each round, a wipe retries the wave. **Native-only:** tap to move or attack, flick to dodge, hold and drag to steer, tap a portrait to lead with that hero; haptics on the leader's hits, crits, blows taken, ultimates and a fall; sounds through AVAudioEngine (it follows the silent switch). **Checked:** built and run in the simulator (iPhone 17 Pro): four waves fought through with the companions on their own, the portrait swap (Pip led with his own buttons; Brann used Bulwark as a companion), a flicked dodge; installed on the user's iPhone 15 Pro Max and launched. **Not checked:** play by hand on the phone (the user is testing it), frame times on the device, the steering drag. **Left out of the slice:** music, saves, gear, levels and XP, the other heroes and foes, the world. |
| 2026-10-31 | **The native prototype: a performance readout, and its first hitches found.** The user asked how to test performance. A two-finger tap shows frames a second against the screen's rate, average and worst frame time, late frames (half again the budget), the game's own update time and nodes; +20 FOES adds a mixed pack to push it. Launched with `-perfLog` (`xcrun devicectl device process launch --console ... -perfLog`) it prints the same every two seconds and adds three packs, so a run on the phone reads on the Mac. **Measured on the user's iPhone 15 Pro Max (Release):** 120 fps steady with the update under 0.5 ms, even at 42 foes, but single frames of 40-90 ms (once 322 ms) in busy fighting; the user's own screenshot showed 92 of 120 with a 53 ms worst. A Time Profiler trace (Instruments, 41 s) put the game's logic at 4.6% of the main thread and rendering at about 6.5%, with no long running update: the spikes were waits. **Fixed, and measured again on the phone (46 s, packs up to 60 foes):** 120 fps held throughout, the update averaging 0.1-1.6 ms with a worst of 5.8 ms, and 5 late frames in all, every one in the two seconds after the first pack of frost wolves, imps and spiders joined (31-42 ms: something done the first time they fight; not yet found). Before the fix the same run had 120 late frames and a 322 ms worst. The fix: each sound stopped the voice it reused with `AVAudioPlayerNode.stop()`, which makes the main thread wait for the audio thread; a sound now cuts in with `.interrupts`. Also every sheet loads at start (a first load mid-fight cost 5-17 ms) and HUD text is rebuilt only when it changes. **Not done:** foes surrounding a hero check every other foe (`surroundSlot`), the biggest steady cost in the logic; fine at 40 foes. |
