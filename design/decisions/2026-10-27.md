| 2026-10-27 | **The Ninth Tower, redesigned as a key object** (approved by the user from a before/after). The old tower was a flat tapering wedge with six windows. Now, shape first: a stepped plinth braced by two great buttresses sloping to the ground, four tiers of shaft each narrower than the one below with an overhanging ledge on corbels and a spike at each end, Malgrim's chamber as a wider drum with a crenellated parapet and torn banners, and a crown of five horns curling round the burning hex. Checked at 3x beside Brann and in place at 844x390 (before and after at the same spot). |

| 2026-10-28 | **The big bosses, hand-built as models** (the user chose this over scaling the puppets by exactly 2x or keeping them hero-sized). The six humanoid bosses were hero-sized puppets scaled up 1.45-1.7x, which gave uneven, chunky pixels. Now each is a 3D model (`gfx/modeler.js`, the creature modeler Keep Defense's ART_GUIDE plans) rendered in all three views at the cast's own pixel size: **Snagtooth** (his snaggle tusk, war paint, a branch spear), **Gutcrusher** (a horned iron helm, a chain, a spiked gauntlet, a great spiked maul), **the Pale Huntsman** (a skull face with cold eyes, an antlered hood, a pelt cloak, a quiver, the longbow drawn and loosed), **Vulk** (red scales, a pale belly, a snouted head under a crown of forged horns, an apron, a tail, the great axe with a forge-hot edge), **Isolde** (a great helm with a crest of ice, frosted plate, a blue tabard and cape, a kite shield with a white tree, the frost lance), **Malgrim** (a long grey-violet coat with a sash of hex-light, a high collar, a pale long face, the bent star-pointed hat, the hex staff; a bolt points it, a cast raises it blazing). Then the creature bosses, also modelled and turning: **Queen Mab**, **Mother Murk** (she never turned before) and **the Cinder Drake** (membrane wings spread to fly). **At the user's note** the first goblins were "at a slight angle vs flat facing onward": the modeler had used the world's 40° camera; the humanoids are now drawn flat-on, like the puppets (10°); the low creatures sit a little under the camera (30°, the Drake 20°) so their backs read. **Loading:** each boss is its own art group, a view a task, loaded with its region or dungeon or built on the spot; nothing is built at boot (the golem's three views had added 84 ms to it until they moved out). **Checked:** turnaround sheets at 3x beside Brann for every boss; each spawned in game turning through its views with no errors; the Undercroft, Abbey, Witchroot, Forge and Spire walkthroughs and the Tower to Malgrim through all five phases (with the Huntsman's and Vulk's Ward Echoes); map tests 9/9. Not checked by hand: a full overworld fight with each, and how every boss reads at phone size in its own arena. |
